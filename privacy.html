Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who runs Logbook

Logbook is run by [your name], [city, country]. Contact: [email address]. For data protection questions, write to the same address.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, a hashed password (we never see the password itself) and your username.
  • What you log: study sessions (date, subject, type, minutes, description), goals, quick logs, resources, scores, Focus sessions and points, and your settings.
  • Social features, only if you use them: friends, friend requests, reactions, blocks, your live Focus status (kept for a few minutes) and, if you switch it on, your hours on community boards.
  • Connections: the tokens you create for trackers (Anki, the browser tracker), and what they send — the site or video title and the minutes for what they log, Anki deck statistics, and, only when you save one yourself, a word with its sentence and page. Page text is never sent otherwise; languages are detected on your computer. If you connect Spotify, we keep the access Spotify gives us (unreadable by the app) and the names of the shows you play.
  • Clans: members of a clan you join see your username, your minutes in that clan's subject(s) this week and month, and kudos. Friends can see if you missed a day so they can save your streak.
  • Error reports: when the app hits an unexpected error it may send the error message, the page and your browser type, so the bug can be fixed.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Why (legal basis)

We process this data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), and error reports to keep it working (legitimate interest). Community boards and showing log descriptions to friends are based on your choice, which you can change at any time in Setup.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in), in the region chosen for the project ([region, e.g. EU – Frankfurt]). The website is served by GitHub Pages. Paintings in Focus mode load from Wikimedia Commons and fonts from Google Fonts, which see your IP address when your browser loads them. Your device also keeps a copy of your logbook so the app works offline.

Who can see what

  • Only you can see your logbook, unless you share it with someone (read-only) in Setup.
  • Friends see your daily totals, subjects, activity types, streak, live Focus status and — only if you allow it — your log descriptions.
  • Community boards show your username and hours per subject, only if you switch them on.

How long

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (Setup → Account → Delete account), your account and everything linked to it are deleted straight away. Backups held by our provider expire within their normal backup cycle.

Your rights

You can see, export (Setup → Export & backup), correct and delete your data yourself at any time. You can also ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing by writing to the contact address above, and you can complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

Logbook is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
